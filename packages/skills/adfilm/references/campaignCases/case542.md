# Blinkwashing (Virgin Mobile · 2014)

## Insight
TODO: Requires full viewing. Likely parodies corporate "purpose washing" — brands adopting superficial social causes to appear values-driven without changing anything. "Blinkwashing" would be a coined term for a new form of cynical brand behavior.

## Mechanic
A satirical campaign that coins the term "blinkwashing" — possibly mocking the speed at which brands rush to attach themselves to trending causes without genuine commitment. Virgin Mobile positions itself against this behavior.

## Why it worked
P11 (absurd as carrier): the satirical premise creates distance from the earnest corporate speak it is mocking. P16: sustained narrative across a campaign gives the joke time to land.
