# Touch (Skittles · 2012)

## Insight
The "Touch the Rainbow" platform gave Skittles a literal mechanic: touch = Skittles = transformation. Any execution simply applies a new object or situation to this formula.

## Mechanic
A short absurdist spot in the Skittles "Touch" series, where contact with Skittles causes an impossible transformation. The exact scenario requires source verification.

## Why it worked
The internal consistency of Skittles' absurdist world means audiences know the rules — and enjoy seeing them applied to new, increasingly strange scenarios. Each "Touch" spot builds brand equity through accumulated surprise.

## Steal
A strong, specific absurdist mechanic (touch transforms) is infinitely extensible. Each new execution is a new application of the same formula — the brand's consistency is the platform.
