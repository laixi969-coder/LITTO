# Pinata (Skittles · 2020)

## Insight
Skittles' long-running absurdist creative platform ("Touch the Rainbow," "Taste the Rainbow") operates through a consistent internal logic: Skittles cause impossible, strange things to happen. Any new execution simply needs a new absurd premise that follows this established logic.

## Mechanic
A short film in Skittles' established absurdist register, featuring a pinata scenario following the brand's surrealist internal logic. Exact execution details require source verification.

## Why it worked
Within Skittles' P11 territory, each execution adds to an accumulated body of absurdist work. The pinata concept fits the established brand grammar.

## Steal
Once a strong absurdist brand platform is established, execution mining is systematic: find everyday objects and scenarios, apply the brand's absurd logic, commit completely.
