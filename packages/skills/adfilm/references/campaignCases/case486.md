# State of Unrest (State Farm · 2021)

## Insight

## Mechanic
A cultural hijack using State Farm's established "Jake from State Farm" character territory — inserting the brand into a moment of cultural tension or unrest with a comedic truth-telling angle. P05 cultural hijack + P13 truth telling suggests the spot used a real cultural moment and commented on it with the brand's characteristic lightness.

## Why it worked
State Farm's campaign revival (2020-2022) demonstrated that insurance brands can achieve genuine pop-culture relevance when they build around a character strong enough to comment on culture rather than just sell product.

## Steal
A well-built brand character (Jake) is an asset that appreciates over time — the character accumulates cultural equity that individual spots cannot build alone. Characters are entry points for cultural conversation that anonymous brand identities cannot access.
