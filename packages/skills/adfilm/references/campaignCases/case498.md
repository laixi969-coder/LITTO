# Color Changes Everything (Target · 2010)

## Insight
TODO: Requires full viewing. Likely explores how color and affordable design can transform the emotional texture of everyday domestic spaces and moments.

## Mechanic
A cinematic film using visual transformation — scenes before and after Target's design and color palette — to argue that accessible design is not a luxury but a tool for changing how life feels.

## Why it worked
P16 (long-form drama) in a retail context is a risk that signals confidence. Target positions itself not as a discount retailer but as a design democratizer. The emotional arc — mundane to vivid — makes the brand argument without a single product claim.
