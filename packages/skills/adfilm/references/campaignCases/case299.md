# Faces (Levi's · 2013)

## Insight

TODO: Year is uncertain — inferred from URL pattern; verify against campaign records.

Faces and individual identity are at the core of denim culture — Levi's wearers are not demographics but distinct people with distinct stories.

## Mechanic

OOH/print campaign using portraiture and design craft to make individual faces the hero of the visual — design as idea, with faces as the graphic language of the brand.

## Why it worked

Design as idea meets craft as message: strong portraiture in print contexts commands attention without headline or concept — the face itself is the argument for individuality.

## Steal

In fashion, the face is the most powerful graphic unit. When the photography is exceptional, no headline competes. Trust the image.
