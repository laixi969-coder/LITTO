# Rainbow (Waitrose · 2011)

## Mechanic
A visually rich short film using the imagery of a rainbow — natural colour, abundance, beauty — to associate Waitrose produce with the best of what nature offers, executed with the production values of a short film rather than a retail ad.

## Why it worked
P16 and P08: emotional storytelling backed by exceptional craft creates a quality signal that no price-based retailer can replicate. Waitrose does not compete on price, so every execution must make the case for quality through form.
